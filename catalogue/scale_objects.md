# Scale objects

Both objects lie flat on the bench top, in the Z=0 plane, and do not move between photos.
Crops are in work/crops_catalogue/ (sq1500_*, ru1496_*).

## Framing square (yellow painted steel)

- **Maker and face:** the heel stamp reads "SWANSON TOOL CO. INC / SPEEDLINE / MADE IN USA / T?-154", with a triangle logo (sq1500_logo.jpg). The model digit before 154 is not legible. This is consistent with Swanson's 16 x 24 in steel framing square.
  - The face showing is the FACE side: the rafter tables are printed on the body ("LENGTH COMMON RAFTERS PER FOOT RUN", "HIP OR VALLEY", "DIFF IN LENGTH OF JACKS 16 IN CENTERS"), with the logo at the heel.
- **Body (long leg), nominal 24" x 2":**
  - The outer-edge numbers reach 23 at the visible end. The inner edge reaches 22.
  - Rafter-table numbers sit under each inch.
- **Tongue (short leg), nominal 16" x 1.5":**
  - The outer-edge numbers are legible to 14, where it passes under the right rocker's rear tip in 1500. The inner edge is legible to 13.
  - It has a centre row of dotted scale marks (e.g. 1|5, 2|10, 3|15; an essex/octagon-type table) and a hang hole near 4 on the outer edge.
  - The widths (2" and 1.5") and lengths (24" and 16") are standard for this model but were NOT measured here. The solve agent should confirm them from the inch numbers, not assume them.
- **Graduations:**
  - Every inch number is legible along both edges in 1500, 1513, 1514 and 1544.
  - Eighth-inch ticks are resolvable at full resolution only in 1500 (near the heel). Sixteenths are not reliable in any frame.
- **Position:**
  - The heel is at the chair's rear-right, on the bench.
  - The body runs forward (+Y) along the right of the chair, outboard of ROCKER_R, ending at about the front-leg station.
  - The tongue runs in -X behind the chair. Its end (about inch 15–16) sits under or just beside the rear tip of ROCKER_R (1500).
  - The face side is up; the outer edges face the right and the rear of the bench.
- **Best photos, fully and sharply visible, best first:**
  - 1500: close, all numbers legible.
  - 1544 and 1545: top-down, whole square in a plane.
  - 1513 and 1514.
  - 1540, 1542, 1538, 1539, 1537.
  - 1501, 1502, 1499.

## Black ruler: "Biscuit Buddy" (essickwoodworks.com, PATENT PENDING)

- **Construction:** a black, centre-zero, centre-finding rule about 1" wide (≈110 px in 1496 at 111 px/in).
  - A pin or screw hole sits at the zero (centre) and another at each "24"/"12" end. The logo ends extend about 2–2.5" past the graduations.
  - The overall length is estimated at about 28–29" (not measured).
- **Scales:**
  - **Lower row:** true inches from the centre, numbered 1–12 each way (24" of graduations in total).
  - **Upper row:** a doubled scale, numbered 1–24 each way, so each upper number sits at half its value in inches. The upper ticks divide each 1/2" into 8, giving 1/16" ticks.
  - **Evidence:** the upper "2" lines up with the lower "1" (ru1496_zoom.jpg). Pixel pitch is 55.5 px per upper unit and 111 px per lower unit. Only the lower-row-equals-inches reading gives a px/in close to the nearby rocker tip and leg widths.
  - **Caution:** the units are inferred, not printed. Confirm with the solve, e.g. against the square, before using the ruler as a primary scale.
- **Legibility:** in 1496 every number on both rows is legible and the 1/16 ticks resolve near the centre. In 1531–1536 and 1525–1529 the numbers are legible; ticks resolve only near the camera.
- **Position:**
  - It lies along the bench's front edge, in front of the chair and parallel to X, roughly centred on the chair (zero near X=0, to check).
  - It is a few inches in front of the front rocker tips.
  - It is also seen edge-on from the left side (1505/1506) and along the near edge in the high left views (1547–1549).
- **Best photos, fully and sharply visible, best first:**
  - 1496: square-on, sharp.
  - 1534, 1535, 1533, 1531, 1536.
  - 1525, 1526.
  - 1527, 1528, 1529.
  - 1473, 1474, 1484, 1485, 1538, 1539.
  - It is partial in 1469–1472, 1477, 1478, 1509, 1510, 1520–1524, 1537, 1547–1549 and 1583.
